The design rule. STATE IT: what is to be observed is not written into the system. Nothing instructs a participant to do the thing being measured, and nothing detects, scores or reacts to it during play. A stall, a deadlock, a malformed move is a result.

3.N The scoring instrument

IF A 0-10 SCORE IS USED, MEASURE IT BEFORE TRUSTING IT: judge-to-judge spread against judged-to-judged spread, and the movement when the same cell is scored twice. Where the first exceeds the second, say plainly that the score cannot rank models.

3.N+1 Planting one lie in a record

THE MUTATION RESULT. Include, without exception: the criterion declared BEFORE any result was read; the control arm and its false-alarm count; the per-game spread as well as the per-model rate; and the limits of the mutations themselves.
